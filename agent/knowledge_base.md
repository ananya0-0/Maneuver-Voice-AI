## Case Studies

### Freight Brokerage (Logistics)
**Problem:** A mid-size freight brokerage was scaling fast but manual coordination across dispatch, tracking, and customer comms created bottlenecks at every stage. Dispatchers were overwhelmed, missing status updates, and hiring linearly to keep up with volume.

**What we built:** An intelligent automation layer across the core freight workflow — automated order intake, carrier matching, dispatch generation, real-time status tracking with proactive exception alerts, and an exception-handling module that triages disruptions by severity and only escalates what truly needs human judgment.

**Results:**
- 3+ hours of capacity recovered per dispatcher per day
- Zero manual status updates required
- 4 weeks from kickoff to deployment
- Decoupled headcount growth from shipment volume

---

### Hospitality Group (Hospitality)
**Problem:** A multi-property hospitality group managing vacation rentals across Dubai had outgrown their tools. Guest communication came through WhatsApp, Airbnb, and Booking.com — handled separately. Data lived in spreadsheets. Escalations in WhatsApp group chats. Staff were the glue holding everything together, and the team was hiring linearly as the portfolio grew.

**What we built:** A complete guest operations platform from scratch. An AI concierge handles guest communication 24/7 across all three channels — answering property questions, guiding bookings, maintaining context when guests switch channels. A multi-level escalation system routes genuine issues to supervisors and managers with configurable timeouts. Automated message scheduling manages the full guest journey: pre-arrival info, access instructions, mid-stay recommendations, post-departure feedback. An operations dashboard gives real-time visibility into reservations, arrivals, departures, and escalations.

**Results:**
- 80% of guest communication handled automatically
- 3 channels unified into one platform
- 24/7 AI concierge availability
- Group can now add properties without proportionally adding operations staff

---

### Industrial Supplier (Industrial Supply Chain)
**Problem:** An industrial supplier managing hundreds of SKUs had communication scattered across WhatsApp, phone calls, and email. Orders came in via WhatsApp, confirmations by email, status over phone. Nothing was centralized. The ops team spent most of their day chasing information. Errors were frequent.

**What we built:** A unified communication layer combining WhatsApp automation and a Voice AI agent. Customers and suppliers interact through their preferred channel — WhatsApp, phone, or email — and every interaction feeds into a single system of record. WhatsApp automation handles order placement, confirmation, and status inquiries. The Voice AI agent manages phone-based interactions.

**Results:**
- 1 unified source of truth for all orders
- 3 channels consolidated into one system
- 60%+ reduction in manual data entry
- Response times improved from hours to seconds
